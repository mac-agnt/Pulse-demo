# Pulse demo script — McGrane Precast

## Start and stop

1. Double-click **Start Demo.command**. Chrome opens at **http://pulse.localhost:8080**.
2. Close that Terminal window when you're done. That stops the demo.

The first time, macOS may ask whether Terminal can access your Downloads folder: click **Allow**.
No internet needed; React and the fonts are bundled in `vendor/`.

## Story numbers (same everywhere)

67 open enquiries · 12 need follow-up · 23 quotes in flight · 41% win rate · 62% yard stock accuracy · 4 overdue quotes >14 days.

## Questions Helios answers

Type these on Home, or click the matching suggestion. The wording can change; Helios listens for the key words.

| Ask | Helios shows | Key words it listens for |
|---|---|---|
| Which quotes need a follow-up call? | 23 in flight, 41% win rate; 4 quotes >14 days with no call | quotes, win rate, India, follow-up call |
| What is overdue in my work? | 67 open enquiries, 12 need follow-up, 23 in the India queue | enquiry, tasks, late, overdue, behind, my work |
| Where is yard stock accuracy this morning? | 62% vs 95% target; hollowcore notebook 14 / floor 9 | stock, yard, accuracy, counts, notebook, herb, health |
| What loads are going out today? | Six loads; Load 05 untagged; forklift idle | load, dispatch, forklift, lorry, loading |
| Log four follow-up calls | A write tool: drafts it and waits for your yes | chase, call, email, draft, send |

Anything else gets: "Everything I reach goes through a registered tool with a declared permission, and none of them covers that question yet." It then offers buttons for the questions above, so click one and carry on.

## Watch out for

- **Chase / log calls:** stop at the **NEEDS YOUR YES** card, which is the point to make. Clicking **Confirm and log** or **Edit scripts** makes Helios draft the same note again.
- **Stay in dark mode.** Light mode still shows some leftover chart tints.
- **Old link:** `localhost:8080/Pulse%20v4%20Glass.dc.html` no longer works. Use http://pulse.localhost:8080.
